Definitions used below
----------------------
* "Experiment duration" is the labeled event/evaluation window in
  filtered_complex_event_experiments.csv. It is the duration that should be
  replay-scoped and counted in an evaluation runtime estimate.
* "Raw recording" is a sensor recording chunk containing one or more experiment
  windows. Raw chunks must not be mistaken for CE duration.
* "Recommended" means recommended_for_use=True in the label catalog.
* An Orin node comprises a fixed ZED stereo/depth camera and normally a ReSpeaker
  microphone. Some campaigns also recorded GPS/status streams.
* Mobile archive numbers are physical archive identifiers, not stable logical
  device or map identities. Handsets were moved/swapped between experiments.

SENSORS, MODALITIES, AND MEDIA FORMATS
======================================

Fixed Orin/ZED nodes, 2024 and 2025
-----------------------------------
* Nominal deployment: orin1 through orin10 (10 fixed devices).
* Modalities found: ZED stereo video/depth, ZED frame-timestamp CSV, and ReSpeaker
  multichannel audio. The label sheet also names vehicle GPS IDs, but the generated
  replay scenario catalog did not find archived GPS files for these labeled dates.
* Exported ZED media measured from the corpus:
    left RGB MP4:  1920 x 1080, H.264, 15 fps
    right RGB MP4: 1920 x 1080, H.264, 15 fps
    depth MP4:     1920 x 1080, 15 fps
    original:      ZED SVO plus timestamp/configuration CSV
* ReSpeaker example measured from the corpus: FLAC, 16 kHz, 6 channels. The files
  are microphone-array audio suitable for audio-event classification and, where
  geometry is known, GCC-PHAT localization.
* Most matched scenarios contain all ten nodes. A few 2024 Route-convoy source
  scenarios contain 9 or only 1 catalogued node, and Two-vehicle-chase source
  chunks contain 8–10. This is media availability, not an assertion that every
  camera sees the CE.

Mobile Android nodes
--------------------
* Six physical archives exist: mobile_archive_1 through mobile_archive_6.
* Each probed archive contains portrait H.264 video at 720 x 1280, approximately
  24–30 fps, with AAC mono audio at 48 kHz.
* Archives 1–3 use named recordings such as recording_<scenario>_<epoch-ms>.mp4
  and cover the earlier collection layout. Archives 4–6 contain timestamp-only
  recording__<epoch-ms>.mp4 files used by the integrated 2025 replay path.
* The topology drawings use deployment-local names n1–n3 (2024) and d1–d3 (2025).
  These names are not safely interchangeable with archive numbers. Replay retains
  archive IDs unless an explicit run-specific alias map exists.
* A phone file contains synchronized image and microphone tracks. Consequently,
  mobile replay can provide both visual person/vehicle evidence and audio evidence
  such as speech/alarm. It should not be treated as camera-only media.

Fixed Orin/ZED nodes, 2026
--------------------------
* Physical node pool: orin11 through orin16 (up to 6 fixed devices).
* Matched experiments have 5–6 catalogued nodes; individual April 13 scenarios
  can have 4–5. The explicit label hints identify orin14 for the first convoy
  tests and orin14/orin16 for two robbery tests, but evaluation may fan out to all
  available selected nodes.
* Modalities found: ZED SVO2 video/depth with frame timestamp CSV, ReSpeaker audio,
  and GPS CSV in many scenarios. GPS availability varies on April 13/15.
* Replay code initializes ZED at HD1080 and the timestamp CSVs show approximately
  15 fps. Unlike 2024/2025, this corpus generally retains SVO2 instead of separate
  left/right/depth MP4 exports.
* The 2026 camera locations were dynamic and have no trustworthy geometric site
  calibration. Relevant node labels may scope search, but 2024/2025 spatial priors
  and topology distances must not be applied to 2026.

2024 — Route convoy (25 experiments; 23 recommended)

data; audio is recorded but is not central to the CE.
Sensors/modalities: nominally Orin 1–10 with ZED + ReSpeaker, plus n1–n3 in the
2024_temporal_ce1 topology. Of the 25 matched fixed-node scenarios, 24 contain
both ZED and ReSpeaker data; one has ZED only. Per-scenario node count is 1, 9, or

RAW-DURATION AND ALIGNMENT CAVEATS
==================================

1. The 2026 generated scenario catalog reports approximately four-hour observed
   durations for many raw chunks (roughly 14,400 seconds). These values result from
   the union of heterogeneous CSV timestamps, especially GPS/status timelines, and
   are not CE recording lengths. The labeled 2026 corpus is 40 minutes total.
2. Nineteen 2024 chase windows are embedded in three longer raw recording chunks.
   Summing those chunks once gives about 50 minutes, while summing the actual labels
   gives 14:26. Evaluation must trim by event time.
3. Android filename epoch milliseconds are converted explicitly from site-local
   America/New_York event times to UTC. A mobile file is selected only when it
   overlaps the event window with sufficient coverage; adjacent five-minute chunks
   may be stitched.
4. Source recordings start before some labeled events. A large difference between
   a scenario filename time and a label start is therefore expected and is not by
   itself clock misalignment.
5. "Sensors present" means media files exist in the indexed source scenario. It
   does not mean every sensor sees or hears the event, nor that all streams have
   complete coverage of the full labeled interval.
6. The label catalog is the source of experiment counts, CE names, quality flags,
   and replay windows. The generated scenario catalog is the source of observed
   fixed-node/modalities. Manual corrections in evaluation/labels/README.md take
   precedence over misleading original spreadsheet names.
